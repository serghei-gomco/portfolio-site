## Context

The platform runs 70+ online stores on one Nuxt 2 / Vue 2 SSR codebase with several themes. On mobile, the
homepage visibly jumped while loading: content moved down as blocks above it appeared.

## The problem in numbers

Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) on the mobile homepage was **0.51**. Google treats anything above **0.25** as
"Poor" and under **0.1** as "Good".

## What I did

- Measured which elements moved during load on mobile and in which order.
- Fixed the causes in the **shared homepage components**, including the mobile hero block — so the fix reached
  most of the platform's storefronts, not just one.
- Re-measured on 8 different stores after the change.

## Result

- CLS went from **0.51 to under 0.06**.
- **All 8 measured stores** are under the 0.1 "Good" threshold.
- These are lab measurements, not field data from real users.
