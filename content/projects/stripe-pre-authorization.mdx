## The request

One store on the platform (a B2B business in the US) asked for a different payment flow: instead of charging the
card at checkout, the payment should be **held** and charged later from the admin — or released.

## What I built

- A **store-level option** "manual capture": when it is on, checkout creates a Stripe payment that is only
  authorized (held), not charged.
- On the **admin order card** a manager can **capture** the held amount or **release** it.
- **Refunds and payment status updates** arrive through Stripe webhooks.
- **Webhook events are isolated per store**: one store's events can never change another store's orders.

## Safety

The option is off by default, so every other store on Stripe kept working exactly as before; this isolation was
checked explicitly. The feature was merged to staging, with the production hotfix in review when I left the company.
